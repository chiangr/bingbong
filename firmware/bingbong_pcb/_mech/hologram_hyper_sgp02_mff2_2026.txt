Hyper eUICC SIM – Embedded format

Product overview                                                                                                                            Primary part number
A secure, device-agnostic IoT embedded SIM card for global deployments built for M2M data, with                                             G3-F-DFN8
easy activation, transparent pricing, and developer-friendly tools. Hologram’s Hyper SIMs use an
eUICC OS to remotely update coverage over-the-air by adding or removing connectivity profiles,
without requiring you to swap the SIM in your device. Network fallback functionality can dynamically
switch profiles in the event that a profile loses connectivity.



Network availability
Works globally with Hologram's 2G through 5G network including LTE-M and NB-IoT where
coverage is available. More information: https://www.hologram.io/pricing/coverage
