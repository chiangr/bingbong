  Type of Connectors

Murata offers a variety of connectors:
• Switch connector to measure RF circuit.
• Board to Board connector to transmit high frequency signals from board to board with low loss.

Microwave Coaxial Connectors with Switch
The RF circuit and ANT characteristics can be measured by
mounting in an RF transmission line.
The internally built-in mechanical switch separates the RF
circuit and ANT circuit, so that the circuit can be measured
without any mutual effect using a dedicated probe made
by Murata. Except when measuring with probe, internal
mechanical switch is connected, so RF circuit and Antenna
circuit are connected.                                                                                                                      RF                                                      ANT
