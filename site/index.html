01 / A touch that travels

A little turn.
A little closer.

Every click of your crown becomes a little touch in their hand. Turn faster, and the clicks become a purr.

24 REAL DETENTSDrag the crown. Find your rhythm.

Ceramic on steel. The click is mechanical, even with a flat battery. The crown stops at the next detent.

The path of a touch +
  1. Your turn Angle sensed 100 times / second
  2. Cellular Up to 10 packets / second
  3. Their hand A 170 Hz haptic actuator

Petting target: ≤250 ms typical, 400 ms ceiling. This demo uses 250 ms. Faster than about 12 clicks per second becomes a purr; it fades over 600 ms when you stop. Sessions taper at 15 seconds and stop at 20 seconds. Design targets, unmeasured.

Away always means away +

Unbonding looks the same as a flat battery: the neutral away state. The creature never becomes sad, hungry or disappointed. At 3% battery, one final going-to-sleep message lets the other creature rest.

Location isn’t an option: the radio’s location pin is left unconnected. If service lapses, the device uses a plain-language service message, never a guilt-inducing creature.

11 / A mechanism in miniature

A good feeling.
Built in.

A cup, a race, two tiny balls. Springs, a magnet, and a sealed press mechanism. Every piece has a job.

Ø13 mm · 48 cut serrations

Crown + integral hub

One metal part rotates and presses. 316L is the reference; final material follows prototype testing.

Made
One bar is CNC turned and bored in a common setup. All 48 serrations are cut, then the part is finished before assembly.
Fitted
The race bonds into the cup from the rear. The integral hub locates the washer, retention ring and sensing magnet.
24-groove race+

Hardened 17-4PH · reference build

24-groove race

24 grooves give 15° steps. Zirconia and BeCu races remain prototype alternatives.

Made
Turned from wrought stock, heat-treated, then wire-EDM cut with 24 internal grooves. The profile is finished and passivated.
Fitted
An annular tool seats it against the step inside the crown. It rotates with the crown.
Two ceramic balls+

Si₃N₄ · Ø0.800 mm

Two ceramic balls

Two balls. Twenty-four grooves. Both balls seat together at each click.

Made
Precision silicon-nitride balls are sourced from a ceramic-ball specialist and checked for size and roundness.
Fitted
Exactly two balls sit in opposed pockets in the static sleeve, each supported by its own leaf spring.
Detent leaf springs+

C17200 BeCu · 0.12 mm strip

Detent leaf springs

The leaves deflect as the balls cross each land, then return them into the next groove.

Made
Etched from strip, formed with a ball-locating dimple, then age-hardened. Installed force is checked by lot.
Fitted
Roots locate beneath the sleeve’s bosses. The free ends press the balls radially into the race.
Static journal sleeve+

POM-C · moulded, then sized

Static journal sleeve

The crown moves. The sleeve stays fixed to the tray.

Made
The sleeve is moulded with pockets and ears. Journal, bore and flange datums are machined in one secondary setup.
Fitted
Slides over the crown hub from the rear. Its two journal lands guide both rotation and the short press stroke.
Wave washer+

BeCu · three-wave return spring

Wave washer

Preloads the crown outward and compresses when you press.

Made
Thin strip is stamped into three waves and age-hardened; the working force is checked.
Fitted
Drops over the hub against the inside front wall of the crown before the sleeve is inserted.
Captive C-ring+

BeCu · Ø0.40 mm wire

Captive C-ring

Keeps the crown captive while leaving room for its 0.40 mm reference press stroke.

Made
Wire is formed into a deburred C-ring and checked for spring-back.
Fitted
Compressed into the hub groove, it springs outward into the sleeve’s groove as the sleeve seats.
Sensing magnet+

NdFeB · Ø6 × 2.5 mm

Sensing magnet

Rotates with the crown; the sensor reads its field through the sealed diaphragm.

Made
A diametrically magnetised disc is inspected and oriented to the crown’s reference mark.
Fitted
Bonded into the hub pocket after finishing and mechanical tests. The pocket lip provides additional retention.
Sealed press diaphragm+

LSR · 0.30 mm skin · PEEK pip

Sealed press diaphragm

Transfers the press while separating the mechanism from the electronics.

Made
Silicone is moulded around a small PEEK core. Pip heights are binned for selective assembly.
Fitted
The rim is clamped at the sleeve flange. The off-axis pip presses the tact switch on the dry side.
Crown satellite board+

Angle sensor · Hall latches · tact

Crown satellite board

Angle sensing is magnetic. The press uses a separate off-axis switch. Sensor selection remains open.

Made
The small circuit board is assembled and electrically tested. Tact height is measured before diaphragm selection.
Fitted
Locates on the sleeve’s pins and connects to the main board by a flex tail.

Reference build: 316L crown, 17-4PH race.
Final crown geometry, materials and optional rotary seal follow prototype trials.

13 / A connection through the details

The cap carries
the connection.

A machined aluminium cap. An optical dividing ring. A gold contact you never see. Together, they complete the antenna assembly.

6061-T6 aluminium · CNC machined

Antenna end cap

The metal cap participates in the cellular antenna with the board’s ground structure. RF performance awaits chamber tests.

Made
The capsule exterior, inner cavity and integral feed tab are machined together. Exterior finishing leaves the contact land protected.
Fitted
Keys onto the optical ring; the joint and sealed tab aperture are bonded before final assembly.
Optical isolation ring+

Optical PC · 1.5 mm visible web

Optical isolation ring

The luminous gap separates the metal cap from the body. The interior web also forms part of the seal.

Made
Moulded in optical polycarbonate with locating pins, a light-injection guide and an aperture for the feed tab.
Fitted
Keys between the tray and cap. The tab passes through its sealed opening; an internal guide brings light from the LED.
Plated feed land+

Nickel barrier · hard-gold contact

Plated feed land

The electrical contact is made to the prepared land; exterior anodising is kept out of this contact.

Made
The contact land is selectively prepared and plated while the exterior is finished. Plating route remains under qualification.
Fitted
The gold land is on the underside of the cap’s integral L-tab, directly above the spring contact.
Spring feed contact+

Plated BeCu · SMT spring finger

Spring feed contact

A compliant, wiping contact joins board and cap. Exact supplier and stack remain prototype decisions.

Made
A plated spring contact is selected against the required working height and force curve, then reflowed to the board.
Fitted
Deflects vertically against the underside of the feed tab as the board seats. The cap’s structural lip carries impact loads.

Six-layer FR4 · 23 × 52 × 0.8 mm

Main circuit board
